The three phases are the ones the Ready contract names, timed per
round rather than per operation: persist covers the truncate, the
append, the hard state and the one fsync over all three; send covers
encoding every message and handing it to the transport; apply covers
the state machine's batch and its own fsync.

Read the two arms against each other. The difference in persist is the
flush, almost exactly; the difference in apply is the state machine's
own flush, and what is left of apply is real work — decoding, the
session table, the store.
